EMERGENCY POTABLE WATER

How an emergency water tanker
delivery works.

From the first phone call to the discharge point, these are the practical details that make an urgent potable water delivery work.

Potable water tankers · Emergency supply · Site delivery

Emergency potable water deliveries are usually arranged because something has changed: a mains interruption, site fault, storage issue, planned works or another situation has left a property without its normal drinking-water supply.

Start with the location and requirement

The first useful details are the delivery address, required timing and approximate quantity. You do not need to know every technical detail before contacting a tanker supplier.

Site access is important

A tanker needs somewhere practical to arrive, position and discharge. Access restrictions, narrow roads, security gates, height limits and the distance to the receiving tank can all affect the delivery.

Know where the water is going

Tell the supplier whether the tanker will discharge into a storage tank, temporary tank or another approved receiving arrangement. The hose route and connection point should be considered before arrival.

Plan for the duration of the interruption

If the normal supply may be unavailable for several days, think about storage capacity and consumption rather than treating the first tanker as the complete solution. Further deliveries may be required.

Emergency delivery checklistLocation · postcode · contact on site · required quantity · required time · tanker access · receiving tank/storage · hose route · expected duration of interruption.

Potable water means water intended for human consumption

UK Drinking Water Inspectorate guidance states that water intended for human consumption includes water supplied from a tanker, and its emergency guidance recognises temporary tanker supply as a possible response in an emergency.

P&S supplies potable water for hotels, airports, commercial premises, construction sites, events and other temporary or emergency requirements.
