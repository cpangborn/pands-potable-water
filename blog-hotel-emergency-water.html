HOTEL EMERGENCY WATER

What to do if your hotel
loses its water supply.

A practical guide for hotel managers, facilities teams and operators dealing with an unexpected potable water interruption.

Emergency potable water · Hotels · UK

A hotel without water can quickly become an operational problem. Guest bathrooms, kitchens, drinking water, staff facilities and cleaning operations may all be affected. If the normal supply cannot be restored immediately, an emergency potable water tanker can be part of the temporary response.

1. Establish what has actually failed

Before arranging an emergency water delivery, establish whether the interruption affects the whole building or only part of the site. Check storage tanks, booster systems, incoming mains supply and any known planned works.

2. Work out how much water you need

The required quantity depends on occupancy, available storage, which facilities remain operational and how long the interruption is expected to last. A rough estimate is better than waiting until the site is completely without water.

3. Check where a tanker can access

Tell the tanker supplier about the hotel's entrance, loading area, access restrictions, height or weight restrictions and the approximate distance from the vehicle to the receiving point. Airport-area hotels can have busy forecourts and controlled vehicle movements, so access planning matters.

Useful information to have readyHotel name or site location, postcode, contact number, required date and time, approximate quantity, available storage, tanker access and where the water needs to be discharged.

4. Think beyond the first delivery

If the underlying fault will take time to repair, one tanker may not be enough. Consider storage capacity and expected consumption so further deliveries can be scheduled before the temporary supply runs out.

Emergency potable water for hotels

UK Drinking Water Inspectorate guidance notes that emergency water may be temporarily supplied by tankers or bottles/containers when a normal supply problem creates a need for alternative water. Water intended for human consumption remains subject to the relevant requirements.

For an urgent hotel water delivery, P&S can discuss the location, quantity, timing and practical site requirements with you.
