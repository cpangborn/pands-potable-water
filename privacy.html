PRIVACY

Your information.
Handled responsibly.

This notice explains what information P&S Potable Water collects through this website and how it is used.

What information we collect

If you contact us through the website, we may collect your name, company name, telephone number, email address, delivery location, required date, approximate water requirement and any other information you include in your enquiry.

How we use your information

We use enquiry information to respond to your request, discuss and arrange potable water deliveries, communicate with you about the requirement and maintain appropriate business records.

Website forms

The enquiry forms on this website are processed using Netlify Forms. Netlify stores form submissions in its systems and provides tools for managing and exporting submissions. Netlify also applies spam filtering to form submissions. See the Netlify Forms documentation for more information.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with the enquiry, provide the requested service and maintain appropriate business records, unless a longer period is required by law.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law gives you rights including access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing and objection to certain processing.
