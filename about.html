THE SERVICE

Keep it simple.

When you need potable water delivered, the important things are straightforward communication, the right tanker for the job and a delivery arrangement that works at your site.

P&S Potable Water is focused on exactly that. We take the details, understand what you need the water for and arrange the supply around your requirements.

Some enquiries are planned days or weeks ahead. Others are made because something has gone wrong and water is needed quickly. Either way, we'll talk through the practicalities rather than making the process complicated.
